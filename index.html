Scaling rules and commercial guardrails
ECONOMICS FIRST

$1,200 illustrative allowable CAC

At assumed $6,000 net AOV and 25% pre-ad contribution, retaining $300 per order leaves $1,200 for acquisition. Break-even is 4x ROAS. Retaining $300 requires 5x.

INCREASE / HOLD / REDUCE

10–15% every 48–72 hours

Increase after mature CAC, contribution, stock and tracking pass. Hold thin evidence. Reduce 20–30% when mature CAC exceeds 1.2 times the approved limit for two reviews, or contribution turns negative.

DIAGNOSE / STOP

Protect loss limits and the journey

Review a test at 1.5 times allowable CAC with no purchase or qualified pipeline, after checking lag. Pause affected spend immediately for broken checkout, duplicate purchases, wrong offers or unavailable stock.

Warm frequency above 4 in 7 days plus CTR down over 20%, or CPM above 1.3 times a comparable baseline alongside a CAC breach, prompts review. These are proposed diagnostics. No universal dollar CPM ceiling is assumed.

CREATIVE AND PRODUCTS

I would start with
eight clear ideas.

The first concepts answer real buying questions: cooking control, product value, fit, a coordinated kitchen, delivery, a genuine owner decision, advice and the approved offer. I would start with two executions of each, then adapt them to placements.

My starting product mix is 60% for 48-inch, 25% for 30/36-inch and 15% for suite tests. Those are labels across the existing media budget, not extra funding. Stock and contribution decide which products qualify. The $6k Meta test reserve is already included.

AI DISCLOSURE

I used AI throughout this assignment, from developing the strategy and comparing alternatives to stress-testing the draft before assembling the full submission. ChatGPT supported strategy development and review. I used Claude 3.5 Sonnet through Python with Instructor and Pydantic to structure and validate model responses. Codex helped organize the project files, build the review website and refine the copy. My research and analysis workflow also included Semrush and Ahrefs free tools, Apify, headless Playwright in Python, Semrush API scripting and Jupyter Notebooks. I treated AI outputs as suggestions to examine. The final submission separates observed evidence, interpretations, assumptions and proposed actions. WHAT I WOULD DO WITH MORE TIME

I would use the extra time to improve the decision.

First, I would repeat the required searches from documented US locations, watch the full competitor videos and look for verified 2025 BFCM ads. Those are public-research gaps I would work on next.

I would then extend the local QA and build a clearly labelled sample dashboard. A prototype needs more time. A useful live dashboard needs approved spend, net orders, refunds and SKU economics as well.

Reconciled dashboard

About 1–2 focused days after access and schema agreement. Show unique orders, contribution, source freshness and the age of enquiry cohorts.

Exception monitor

About 1–2 days after approved exports are available. Route stale stock, price mismatches and pacing exceptions to a person, with retries and visible failures.

Assisted-sale matching

About 2–3 days with CRM and call access, consent rules and test outcomes. Link qualified enquiries to completed sales and keep unmatched records visible.

I would start manually, make the data dependable and automate the repeated checks afterward. None of these connected systems is described as completed. Section B has the proposed owners and acceptance checks.
